NexusDrive Privacy Policy

NexusDrive (operated by ByteNexus at https://nexusdrive-mw.netlify.app/) respects your privacy and operates with a strict Zero-Server Storage architecture.

🔒

100% Zero-Data Storage Promise

NexusDrive runs entirely inside your web browser. We do NOT operate any backend database that stores, reads, copies, logs, or sells your Google Drive files, folders, passwords, or personal credentials.

1. Information We Access

When you choose to sign in with Google or connect your Google Drive, NexusDrive uses Google Identity Services OAuth 2.0 to request only the permissions you explicitly approve:

• Basic Profile (openid, email, profile): Your name, email address, and profile photo to display your active session in the dashboard.
• Google Drive Access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): Strictly used to list, copy/clone, upload, create folders, preview, or trash files/folders when triggered directly by your actions.

2. How Your Data Is Used

All API calls travel directly over encrypted HTTPS from your browser to Google APIs (www.googleapis.com). Your data is used exclusively to perform the Drive actions you initiate inside the NexusDrive dashboard (such as server-side cloud file copying, direct file uploads, or folder management). We never use your data for advertising, analytics profiling, or AI/ML training.

3. Session Security & Retention

Your temporary OAuth access token is encrypted client-side using Web Crypto (AES-GCM) inside your browser's temporary sessionStorage and is automatically wiped when you log out, disconnect your Drive, or close the browser tab. NexusDrive retains zero copies of your Google Drive files or tokens on external servers.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any Google user data or file metadata to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Data only communicates between your browser and official Google API endpoints.

5. Google API Limited Use Compliance

NexusDrive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking Access & Contact

You can revoke NexusDrive's access to your Google account at any time by clicking Disconnect in the dashboard or visiting your Google Account Permissions page.
