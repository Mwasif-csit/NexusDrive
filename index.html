About ByteNexus & NexusDrive

Built for Speed. Engineered for Privacy.

NexusDrive is developed by ByteNexus to solve one simple problem: moving large folders and files between Google Drive accounts without wasting internet data or hours of waiting.

🔒

Our 100% Zero-Data Storage Promise

NexusDrive does NOT store, read, or save any of your personal files or passwords. Everything stays completely private inside your own Google Drive, and transfers happen directly in the cloud with end-to-end encrypted connections.

1. Why Is NexusDrive So Fast?

Instead of downloading files to your device and uploading them again, NexusDrive moves files directly between cloud drives — saving your time and 100% of your internet data.

2. How Is Your Account Protected?

You sign in securely using official Google verification. Your credentials are never shared with us, and your session is automatically cleared whenever you log out or disconnect.

3. What Data Do We Collect?

None. We do not keep copies of your files, we do not track your private folders, and we never sell or share user activity. You remain in full control at all times.

4. About ByteNexus

ByteNexus builds clean, fast, and privacy-focused cloud tools designed to make everyday digital work simple and stress-free on both desktop and mobile.
