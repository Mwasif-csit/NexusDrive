About ByteNexus & NexusDrive

Built for Speed. Engineered for Privacy.

NexusDrive is created by ByteNexus to make moving folders and files between Google Drive accounts effortless, ultra-fast, and completely private.

🔒

100% Zero-Data Storage Promise

NexusDrive does NOT store, read, or save any of your personal files or passwords. Your data remains strictly inside your own Google Drive, and transfers occur directly in the cloud over encrypted connections.

1. Why Is NexusDrive So Fast?

Instead of downloading files to your device and re-uploading them, NexusDrive copies files directly inside the cloud — saving your time and 100% of your internet bandwidth.

2. How Is Your Account Protected?

You sign in securely using official Google verification. Your credentials are never shared with us, and your session is cleared whenever you log out or disconnect.

3. What Data Do We Collect?

None. We do not keep copies of your files, we do not track your private folders, and we never sell or share user activity. You stay in complete control.

4. About ByteNexus

ByteNexus builds lightweight, reliable, and privacy-focused cloud productivity tools designed to work smoothly on both desktop and mobile devices.
