About ByteNexus & NexusDrive

Built for Speed. Engineered for Privacy.

NexusDrive is developed by ByteNexus to make organizing, uploading, and cloning Google Drive folders effortless, ultra-fast, and completely private.

⚡

1000× Direct Cloud-to-Cloud Architecture

Instead of downloading gigabytes to your phone or PC and uploading them again, NexusDrive triggers official server-side copy commands directly between Google's servers — preserving your bandwidth and cloning full folder hierarchies in seconds.

1. Smart Scanning & Auto-Filtering

Paste any shared Google Drive folder link to scan every subfolder cleanly. Built-in Nexus Cleaner automatically filters out promo files, ad links, and junk text files before copying.

2. Exact File Replacement & Injection

Need to swap promo PDFs or watermark files with your own? Specify exact file names to skip and attach your custom files — NexusDrive automatically places them into the exact matching subfolders.

3. Resumable Batch Uploads

Upload multiple files directly from your computer or mobile with real-time progress tracking using Google Drive's official multipart and resumable upload pipeline.

4. Zero-Server Privacy Guarantee

Your OAuth token is encrypted in temporary browser memory using Web Crypto AES-GCM. We never run backend storage servers, never copy your files externally, and never sell user data.
